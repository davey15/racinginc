## 1. Brand essence

**Racing Inc. Media** is a live F1 show, blog and newsletter about **the money, power and speed behind the sport**, hosted by two smart, funny women with real insider access.

> **Tagline:** *Where money, power, and speed collide.*

**Positioning in one line:** the F1 show for people who want to understand how the sport really works, and want to laugh while they learn it.

**What makes us different**
- **Insider access.** Chloe is an engineer. Her husband was Max Verstappen's engineer at Red Bull, and he and Max are close. That gives us real perspective and, with permission, real sources.
- **Brains plus snark.** Chloe brings the engineering qualifications; Vanessa has a political science degree from Harvard. Together they can go deep on the economics, politics and technology of F1, and they never let it get dry.
- **The business of racing.** Budgets, cost caps, contracts, sponsorship, team politics and power struggles are as much our turf as lap times.
- **Not another engineering channel.** We respect the technical channels. We are the funny, sharp friends who explain the same story over a drink.

## 2. Audience

Anyone who appreciates F1, from casual fan to die-hard, who wants entertainment and laughs along with insight. Not a technical deep-dive audience. We assume curiosity, not expertise.

## 3. Voice and tone

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Quick, witty, confident | Mean about drivers' or teams' private lives |
| Insider-informed but plain-spoken | Jargon-heavy or gatekeeping |
| Warm and conversational (two friends talking) | Corporate or press-release stiff |
| Opinionated, with receipts | Rumour-mongering or clickbait |

**Rules of thumb**
1. Explain any technical or financial term in one sentence, with a joke if one fits.
2. Take positions, and say out loud which parts are fact and which are opinion.
3. Humor punches up at situations, institutions and ourselves, not down at individuals.
4. Anything from inside sources is used only with permission. **NDA status: the hosts are checking.** Until they confirm, don't name sources or relay team-confidential detail.

**Headline and caption style:** short, punchy, sentence case for readability; ALL CAPS only in logos, labels and big graphic text. No clickbait that the video doesn't deliver.

## 4. Logo

The logo is the custom artwork supplied by David (`brand/logo/source/racinginclogo.pdf`). It is vector and must be used as supplied: **do not retype, redraw, restretch or recolor it.** It is a bold, forward-leaning wordmark spelling RACIN with a custom red **G** as the final letter.

### 4.1 Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo/racing-inc-logo.svg` | **Primary.** Off-white + red, for dark backgrounds |
| `logo/racing-inc-logo-on-light.svg` | Black + red, for white or light backgrounds |
| `logo/racing-inc-logo-white.svg` | One-color white (watermarks, video overlays, embroidery) |
| `logo/racing-inc-logo-black.svg` | One-color black (single-ink print, stamps) |
| `logo/racing-inc-symbol.svg` | The red G alone (transparent) |
| `logo/racing-inc-avatar.svg` | The G on a black square, for avatars and the favicon |

All files are transparent-background SVGs except the avatar. Export PNGs from the SVGs as needed; never rebuild the logo from a screenshot.

### 4.2 Primary background
Black (`#0A0A0B`). The off-white letters need a dark field; on white or light imagery switch to the on-light version.

### 4.3 Clear space and minimum size
- **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least **half the logo's height** on all sides, free of text, edges and graphics.
- **Minimum width:** 120 px on screen / 25 mm in print for the full logo. Below that, use the symbol.
- **Symbol minimum:** 16 px (favicon).

### 4.4 The symbol (G)
The red G is the final letter of the wordmark (open item 2 asks whether it carries extra meaning) and works as the favicon, avatar and watermark. In circular avatars keep the G inside the central ~60% of the circle (the avatar file is built this way).

### 4.5 Tagline lockup **(proposed)**
Logo with *WHERE MONEY, POWER, AND SPEED COLLIDE.* centred beneath it in the display font, wide-tracked (+0.3 em), silver (`#9AA0A6`), roughly 1/8 the logo's height. Use on the banner, trailers and the website hero. Drop the tagline on avatars, thumbnails and anywhere it would sit below ~12 px.

### 4.6 Don'ts
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate or outline the logo.
- Don't recolor the red or the off-white, and don't use the red logo on a red background.
- Don't add shadows, glows or gradients.
- Don't place it on busy photos without a dark scrim.
- Don't separate the G from the letters in the full logo.
- Don't pair the logo with F1, team or driver logos, which we don't own.

## 5. Color

Black-first, with a single red accent taken from the logo.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Black** | `#0A0A0B` | Primary background |
| **Carbon** | `#17181A` | Cards, panels, surfaces |
| **Graphite** | `#5B6066` | Borders, dividers, checker mid-tone |
| **Silver** | `#9AA0A6` | Secondary text, captions, tagline |
| **Off-white** | `#F9F9F9` | Logo letters, primary text on dark |
| **Racing Red** | `#BE1E2D` | The G, CTAs, "LIVE" badges, one highlight word |

**Proportions:** roughly 70% black/carbon, 20% off-white/silver, 10% red. Red is an accent, never a background fill for large areas.

**Contrast:** off-white and silver on black pass WCAG AA for body text. **Racing Red on black is about 3.2:1**, so use it for large text and graphics only, never for small copy. For small red-ish labels use off-white on a red chip instead.

**A note on red in F1:** Ferrari and F1 itself use red. We stay distinct by being black-dominant and using red sparingly. Don't use Ferrari's or F1's exact reds.

## 6. Typography

The logo is custom lettering. Supporting type should echo its **wide, bold, forward-leaning energy** without competing with it.

| Role | Font | Settings | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** (headlines, graphic text, labels) | **Anybody** (variable, SIL OFL) | width 125–150, weight 800–900, **italic**, uppercase | Free for commercial use; self-host. The italic echoes the logo's slant. |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter** (SIL OFL) | 400 regular, 600 emphasis | Readable at any size. Display face is too wide for paragraphs. |
| **Labels / captions** | Anybody | width 100, weight 600, uppercase, +0.2 em tracking | Small, wide-tracked caps |

Font files used by the visual edition are in `assets/fonts/`.

**Scale (web, proposed):** H1 clamp 36–64 px · H2 28–40 px · H3 20–24 px · body 17–18 px/1.6 · label 12–13 px.

**Alternative:** if the team wants the exact look of the "Brigends Expanded" reference, it's a $29 commercial font; check its license for logo and web use before buying. It would replace Anybody for display only.

**About the F1 reference fonts:** the specimens shared (labelled F1 Turbo / Torque / Regular) are inspiration only. Their license can't be confirmed, so we ship open-licensed fonts instead.

## 7. Imagery and layout

- **Hosts' photography:** black-and-white portraits on a checkered-flag background (the current cover art). Keep the crop, contrast and background identical for both hosts. A colour version can use a red-tinted shadow, never red skin tones.
- **Texture:** the checkered flag, at low contrast, never behind body text.
- **No dead space.** David's design instinct, which we make a rule: tight crops, tight letter-spacing, elements that interlock (like speed bars filling corners) rather than floating in empty frames.
- **Thumbnails:** one face, big emotion, 3 words maximum in the display font, off-white with a single red highlight word. Logo small in a corner.
- **Cleanup needed on the draft cover:** it contains a faint vertical "HOLLYWOOD" watermark between the portraits. Remove it, and confirm rights to both portraits and the flag image.
- **Third-party marks:** F1 logos, team logos, driver likenesses and race footage are other people's property and never form part of our identity. Use them in editorial context only, following fair-use practice.

## 10. Website and data (technical)

The site will be custom-built. **Supabase** holds our data and **Railway** hosts it, so we own the audience relationship and the work we give fans. Design tokens are in `tokens.css`, mirroring sections 5 and 6, so the site, graphics and newsletter share one palette.

Supabase project settings decided: enable Data API; **uncheck** "Automatically expose new tables"; **check** "Enable automatic RLS". Keep the `service_role` key server-side only.

## 11. Legal and rights checklist

- NDA terms and source-naming rules (hosts confirming).
- Trademark search for "Racing Inc." (a generic-sounding name) and for the logo, before heavy investment.

- Fair-use guidelines for F1 footage and imagery.
