01 · Essence

Smart. Funny.
Inside the paddock.

Racing Inc. Media is a live F1 show, blog and newsletter about the money, power and speed behind the sport, hosted by Vanessa Castro and Chloe Wooten.

Insider access

Chloe is an engineer, and her husband was Max Verstappen's engineer at Red Bull. Real perspective, and with permission, real sources.

Brains + snark

Engineering qualifications on one side, a Harvard political science degree on the other. Deep, then hilarious.

Business of racing

Budgets, contracts, politics, power. Not another engineering channel; the funny, sharp friends who explain it.

Proportion

70% black / carbon
20% off-white / silver
10% red

Contrast: off-white and silver on black pass WCAG AA for body text. Racing Red on black is about 3.2:1, so it's for large text and graphics only. Use off-white on a red chip for small labels.

05 · Voice

Two friends who know too much.

We areWe are not
Quick, witty, confidentMean about private lives
Insider-informed, plain-spokenJargon-heavy or gatekeeping
Warm and conversationalCorporate or press-release stiff
Opinionated, with receiptsRumour-mongering or clickbait
  • 1. Explain every technical or financial term in one sentence, with a joke if one fits.
  • 2. Take positions, and say which parts are fact and which are opinion.
  • 3. Punch up at situations and institutions, never down at individuals.
  • 4. Insider information is used only with permission. NDA status is being confirmed; until then, no naming sources.

06 · Imagery & layout

No dead space.

Tight crops, tight letter-spacing, elements that interlock instead of floating. Black-and-white host portraits against a checkered flag, same crop and treatment for both hosts. The flag is a faint texture, never behind body text.

  • Thumbnails: one face, big emotion, 3 words max in the display font, one red highlight word, small logo in a corner.
  • Cleanup: remove the faint “HOLLYWOOD” watermark from the draft cover, and confirm rights to the portraits and the flag image.
  • Third-party marks: F1, team and driver marks are never part of our identity.
